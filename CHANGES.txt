DUSKWARD REALMS - UPDATE 6 (2026-09-17)
=======================================

The tags become a working system, and the records learn to
rabbit-hole. Extract over your existing duskward-realms folder.
Nothing to delete; node_modules untouched; no package.json change.

1. THE TAG SIEVE (BestiaryCabinet.jsx)
   Every entry on every shelf now carries its taxonomy tags - not
   just the two written records - and each cabinet page opens with a
   sieve bar: pick any marks (origin, habitat, behavior, threat,
   rarity, region, folklore) and only the records bearing all of them
   remain. A live count tells you how much shelf is left
   ("2 of 6 records"), selected marks light up gold, and one click on
   "Loosen the sieve" shows everything again. The sieve reads and
   writes the URL, so a filtered shelf is a link you can share:
   /bestiary/general?tag=origin:spirit

2. TAGS ARE TUNNELS NOW (BestiaryBeast.jsx + rows)
   - A record's tag chips link back to that record's cabinet, pre-
     sieved by that mark. Click threat:moderate on Phookas and you
     land on the General shelf showing only its fellow moderate.
   - The chips under every shelf row do the same, toggled in place.
   - The 18 recordless rows carry provisional tags, drafted by the
     archive from your own lore (Bestiary.txt) so the sieve works
     across the whole shelf. They are flagged in bestiary.js as
     awaiting your veto; when you write a record with its own tags,
     the row's array is dropped and yours stand alone.

3. RABBIT-HOLE LINKS (RichText.jsx + both records)
   Bold is now reserved for labels ("Threat Level:") - all decorative
   bolds are unmarked (goblins, knockers, gremlins, take, relentless,
   emotions are plain text again). In their place, a new [[...]]
   marker bolds the name of another entry and leads to its page:
   - Phookas -> Grimlings, the Gloaming's children (the wound-sites
     shelf), the Twilight Deeps (the World), the Fey (Peoples),
     Bogart and Nixies in the cross-references.
   - Grimlings -> Drumen (Peoples, first mention per section), the
     Grey Rot (the Wounding), Bogart and the Sighs in the cross-
     references.
   Records not yet written (Bogarts, Nixies, the Sighs) stand bold in
   bronze with a hover note - the tunnel opens the day their record
   lands under the same slug. The convention is documented in the
   RichText header.

4. THE AUTHOR'S RULINGS APPLIED
   - "Threat Level for Hunters:" -> "Threat Level:" (value keeps your
     hunter-oriented Moderate; the threat tag now reads threat:moderate
     so tag and body agree).
   - Grimlings Folklore Origin ends after two paragraphs: the
     comparison with the real-world originals is gone. The archive
     does not prove itself the stranger.
   - Phooka folklore: unchanged, per your standing audit - the Irish
     puca, the pwca/puck cognates, the black horse, leading travelers
     astray, and the cream offerings all verify as written.

5. SEARCH + CHRONICLE
   - searchIndex: all 18 entry rows gain keywords (folk names + their
     tag ids), so "talisman" finds the Jiang Shi, "hearth" the Bogart;
     Phookas keywords sync to threat:moderate.
   - changelog: new entry "2026-09-17-rabbit-holes".
