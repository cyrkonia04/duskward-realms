1. THE NIXALKA (new record, /bestiary/general/nixalka)
   The third full record, your text verbatim. They take the shelf
   seat the provisional Nixies row held: the folkloric root moves
   from the Germanic nix to the Slavic rusalka (verified - the
   unsettled soul of a woman drowned unjustly, luring the living
   after her), and the creature is reborn wound-born: the water's
   memory of a drowning, wearing the drowned like a mask. Two
   variants (The Deep Ones, The Song-Wrought), the full encounter,
   and cross-references to the Phookas and to the River-Mothers.
   Tags as you listed them: origin:wound-born, behavior:predator,
   habitat:water, threat:high, rarity:uncommon, folklore:slavic -
   the shelf row carries none of its own now; yours stand alone.

2. THE PHOOKAS REMADE (/bestiary/general/phookas)
   Narrative fragments: stories that leaked from the Twilight Deeps,

   their tale requires. Folklore Origin, Physical Description,
   Habitat and the encounter carry over; Nature, Abilities
